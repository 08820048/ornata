# Find your way around

## The command palette

**⌘K** is the way to almost everything. Type part of a note's title or a word inside it to jump there, or the name of a command: "Link graph", "Tags", "Open tasks", "Library", "What's new".

**⌘F** finds text inside the current note.

## Tabs and split view

Every note opens in a tab; **⌘W** closes it, **⌘[** and **⌘]** go back and forward. Press `⌘⇧\` to open a note beside the current one; with several panes open, **⌘1** to **⌘4** move between them.

## The sidebar

Drag notes and folders to reorganize them. They are real folders on disk. Right-click for more. `⌘\` hides the sidebar.

## The library

**⌘K** → "Library" shows the current folder as cards: folders hold their latest notes, and notes show their first page. Click a folder to step in.

## Daily notes

**⌘J** opens today's note, a fresh page for whatever the day brings.

## Focus mode

**⌘⇧F** hides everything except the page. The line you're writing stays in the middle of the window and the rest fades back; you can turn that off in Settings → Editor.

## History

Ornata keeps snapshots of a note as it changes. Open them from the history button in the status bar to compare or restore an older version.
