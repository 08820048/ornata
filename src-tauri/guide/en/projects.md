# Projects and tasks

A project is a folder of notes whose task lines show up as a board. Nothing is stored anywhere else: the board is your Markdown.

## Make a project

Right-click a folder in the sidebar and choose "Set as project". Its tasks, from every note in the folder, appear in the projects tab (**⌘K** → "Open project space"), as a list or a board.

The folder [Example project](note:Getting started/Example project/Tasks.md) next to this note is already one. Open the projects tab to see it.

## A task line

```markdown
- [/] Write the launch post #writing !high @2026-12-01
  - Keep it under 280 characters
```

- The box is the status: `[ ]` to do, `[/]` in progress, `[x]` done, `[-]` canceled
- `#tag`, a priority (`!urgent`, `!high`, `!medium`, `!low`) and a due date `@YYYY-MM-DD`
- Bullets indented under a task are its notes

## On the board

Drag a card to another column to change its status, or onto another card to reorder. Right-click a card to change its priority, copy it for an agent or turn it into a page of its own. "+" at the top of a column adds a task.

Select text in any note and choose "Create issue" to send it to a project, with a link back.
